Hands-On Guide

Step-by-Step Workshop Walkthrough

Follow these 10 steps during our live class session. Look at the animated side indicator as we progress!

1

Create a Free Lovable Account & Start Project

🌐

Visit lovable.dev, log in with your email or GitHub account, and click the "New Project" button.

💡
Why this matters: Setting up your project workspace first gives the AI a clean canvas with modern defaults and instant live preview capabilities.
2

Understand the Prompt Template Fields

🧭

Review the master prompt below: identify where your Name, Tagline/Role, Bio, Skills, Projects, and Contact links fit.

💡
Why this matters: AI generates better output when you provide clear structured data categories rather than a vague one-line request.
3

Draft Your Details in a Notes App First

📝

Open Notepad, Google Docs, or Apple Notes. Write 2-3 honest sentences about yourself and list 2-3 things you've made or are currently studying.

💡
Why this matters: Writing your thoughts peacefully beforehand prevents rushing and avoids wasting AI credits on typo fixes.
4

Copy the Master Prompt from This Guide

📋

Scroll down to the Master Prompt section. You can either fill in the live builder inputs to auto-fill or copy the standard template.

💡
Why this matters: Our master prompt is pre-tuned with aesthetic styling, layout sections, and responsiveness instructions so you get a top-tier design on try #1.
5

Paste Your Filled Prompt into Lovable AI

📥

Paste your customized prompt into the chat box at the bottom of your Lovable project workspace. Make sure no placeholder brackets like [YOUR NAME] were left empty!

💡
Why this matters: Accurate prompt input ensures every card and headline displays your real information immediately.
6

Click Generate & Watch the Magic Happen

⚡

Hit Enter or click Generate. Relax for 20-30 seconds while Lovable builds the HTML structure, styles the soft-UI cards, and renders your website.

💡
Why this matters: Observing the live code generation helps you appreciate how AI orchestrates modern web components behind the scenes.
7

Review Your Site (Desktop & Mobile)

🔍

Click around your new site. Test links, scroll through sections, and toggle between Desktop and Mobile preview icons in Lovable to check responsiveness.

💡
Why this matters: Most recruiters and peers will view your portfolio on mobile phones, so verifying layout flow is essential.
8

Ask for ONE Small Refinement

🎨

Want to tweak a color or text size? Type one specific instruction, e.g.: "Make the hero name heading slightly larger and change the button background to deep navy."

💡
Why this matters: Single-instruction refinements prevent confusing the AI model and keep your site layout completely stable.
9

Click Publish to Get Your Live Link

🚀

Once satisfied with your design, click the "Publish" button located at the top-right corner of Lovable to generate a public .lovable.app URL.

💡
Why this matters: Publishing deploys your project to global edge servers so anyone in the world can visit it with zero hosting fees.
10

Share Your Portfolio with the Class & Beyond

🎉

Copy your live link and drop it into our classroom shared doc/chat! Add it to your email signature, LinkedIn profile, or resume.

💡
Why this matters: Sharing creates peer feedback, builds confidence, and gives you a tangible asset on day one of your college journey.
Master Prompt

The Engineered Portfolio Prompt

A prompt is your set of instructions to the AI. Every single phrase in this prompt tells Lovable what layout, colors, and features to construct.

✨ Quick Prompt Customizer (Optional)

Type your details below to see the prompt text auto-update in real time, or copy the template directly!

PORTFOLIO MASTER PROMPT 
Build a single-page personal portfolio website with a playful yet professional feel. MY DETAILS: Name: [YOUR NAME], Tagline: [YOUR ROLE], Bio: [2-3 sentences], Skills: [list], Projects: [up to 3 with name, description, link], Contact: [email/social links]. Include Hero, About, Projects, and Contact sections. Use a light blue and dark blue color palette with white background, neomorphic soft-UI cards, clean system fonts, subtle scroll animations, and full mobile/laptop responsiveness. Keep the code lightweight and efficient.

Why This Prompt Works (Anatomy Breakdown)

🎭 Tone & Feel

"Playful yet professional" instructs AI to use friendly typography and clean cards instead of boring corporate tables.

🎨 Color & Aesthetic

"Light & dark blue palette, neomorphic soft-UI" prevents plain harsh colors and enforces soft shadows with modern depth.

🧱 Structure & Sections

"Hero, About, Projects, Contact" guarantees standard navigation structure without omitting critical parts.

📱 Technical Quality

"Subtle scroll animations, full mobile responsiveness" ensures it looks crisp on laptops, iPads, and phones.

Best Practices

Tips & Common Beginner Mistakes

Keep these in mind to save time, conserve credits, and get the cleanest portfolio possible.

✅ Do This

Ask for ONE change at a time

When refining, give a single focused instruction (e.g. "change header text"). It executes faster and prevents unexpected layout bugs.

Keep your bio short and authentic

2-3 sincere sentences about what you're learning and building resonate far better than generic buzzwords.

Preview both mobile and desktop

Use the Lovable preview switcher to verify font sizes and button tap targets on smaller phone screens.

⚠️ Avoid This

Don't ask for 10 changes in one prompt

Overloading the AI in a single message often leads to hallucinations or skipped instructions, wasting your generations.

Don't leave placeholder text behind

Always double check that placeholders like [YOUR NAME] or [list] were replaced with real info before publishing.

Don't share the editor URL instead of published link

Make sure you click Publish and copy the public live URL, not your private project workspace link.
